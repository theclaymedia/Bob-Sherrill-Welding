What we do

Fabrication and welding for the job in front of you

From one-off repairs to structural packages, the work is laid out, fit and welded in our shop on Fruitvale Avenue.

  • 01

    Structural steel fabrication

    Beams, columns, moment frames, stairs, ladders, platforms and support steel for commercial, agricultural and industrial buildings. Fabricated to your engineer's drawings and delivered ready to set.

  • 02

    Steel plate fabrication

    Base plates, gussets, brackets, tanks, hoppers and heavy plate parts cut, formed and welded to spec. Carbon steel, stainless and aluminum, in thicknesses from sheet up to heavy plate.

  • 03

    Welding and cutting

    MIG, TIG, stick and flux-core welding on steel, stainless and aluminum. Oxy-fuel and plasma cutting for parts, blanks and demo work. Shop work or on site around Kern County.

  • 04

    Repairs and custom work

    Broken equipment, cracked frames, trailer and hitch repair, gates, railings, truck racks and the one-off parts nobody else wants to make. If it is metal and it is broken, bring it by.

Established1977

A small shop that has outlasted the big ones

Bob Sherrill Welding has been fabricating and repairing steel out of the same corner of Bakersfield for nearly five decades. It is still a small shop, which means the person you talk to on the phone is the person who lays out, fits and welds your job.

We work with contractors, farmers, oilfield service companies and homeowners who need something built correctly the first time. No sales staff, no runaround, just straight answers and clean welds.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, stop by the shop or send the form below. Drawings, photos and rough sketches all help.

  2. Get a straight quote

    We look at the job, ask the questions that matter and give you a price and a realistic timeline.

  3. We build it

    Material is cut, fit and welded in our shop. You are welcome to come check on it as it comes together.

  4. Pick up or delivery

    Pick it up at the shop or we can deliver and install locally, depending on the job.
